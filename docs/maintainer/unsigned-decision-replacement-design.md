# Replace an unsigned prepared V5 decision

Status: implemented in draft PR #94; synthetic upgrade and local recovery tests are included. Exact late-stage ceremony qualification and required release review remain pending. No production ceremony state, proof-tool pin, or published release is changed by this document.

## Problem and exact recovery boundary

A coordinator with a signed V5 final release can complete the guided questionnaire and prepare `ceremony/public/decision/decision.json` before noticing that its derived outcome is wrong. Today `D → 1` refuses to prepare again once that file exists. For the SecondFi ceremony, signed update 26 already records the final release, while the unsigned prepared decision says NO-GO. Its source, rehearsal, deployment, host, randomness, and cleanup gates are PENDING. The final answer shown to the operator as `No` was converted to `Unknown` because `final.findings` remained `Not assessed`. Review dates also require the exact `YYYY-MM-DD UTC` format; prose such as `Sep 27, 2026 UTC` becomes `Not established`. These are material answer-to-report transformations, not a failure of the final-release mathematics.

The requested recovery is narrow: let the coordinator prepare a fresh decision for the **same authenticated final-release checkpoint** before anyone has signed, exported, or sent the active decision. Preserve every earlier unsigned generation and the answers that produced it. The same guarded action may be used again if a newly prepared replacement is also still local and unsigned. The feature does not revise signed ceremony history, replace a final release, or turn incomplete human reviews into PASS.

## Decision and user flow

Add `D → 8) Replace my unsigned prepared decision` to the V5 coordinator guide, shown only after the final release is authenticated and a guided decision exists. The action first performs the eligibility checks below for the **currently active** decision, even if an older generation has already been retired. Its review screen shows the current decision outcome, SHA-256, all non-PASS gates, the exact questionnaire fields that were downgraded during V2-to-V5 expansion, and a list of files that will be retired. It explains that a replacement gets a new timestamp and digest; every earlier decision remains preserved as an unsigned draft and is not an approval. Require `REPLACE UNSIGNED DECISION`, then create a fresh editable questionnaire generation. Do not automatically prepare or sign it.

Start the new questionnaire with the previous *raw* V2 answers as editable suggestions, clearly marked `copied from retired unsigned decision`. Rebind against the current authenticated ceremony, candidate, final checkpoint, signed policy, coordinator identity, and accepted contribution list. If any binding differs, refuse the replacement. The operator must review every answer and may use `:back`, `:save`, and `EDIT ANSWERS` as before. At the final review, display each derived gate and an explicit table of raw answer → expanded value for every downgrade to `Unknown` or `Not established`, with the reason and the original question. Offer `PREPARE GO DECISION` only when the derived outcome is GO. For a candid NO-GO, require `PREPARE NO-GO` and list the remaining FAIL/PENDING gates. This prevents Enter on a default or a generic confirmation from freezing an unexpected outcome.

Add a `What remains for GO` view before preparation. It names every FAIL/PENDING gate, the exact source question(s), the current raw answer and derived value, and the specific condition needed to pass that gate. For example: `source-release — reviewer/date is missing; enter who actually reviewed the pinned source and a date in YYYY-MM-DD UTC`; `formal-go-no-go-checklist — final.withhold was No, but final.findings is Not assessed, so the expanded answer is Unknown; record actual findings and limits`; `participant-entropy — these three accepted contributions have no accepted randomness review; review their real evidence or leave them Unknown`. A numeric gate selection jumps to the relevant questionnaire section, avoiding a restart through every contribution. After each edit, regenerate the preview and update the list. If every required gate is PASS and policy-optional gates are NOT_REQUIRED, label the result `Mechanically derived GO preview — not approval`; pinned proof-tool checks and each signer's independent review of the evidence still precede signing. The view must never say that typing a positive choice proves a review happened, suggest fabricated findings, or relax an evidence requirement. If a review did not happen or revealed a blocker, the truthful answer remains `Not reviewed`, `Unknown`, `No`, or blocked as appropriate, and the outcome remains NO-GO.

Within a selected gate, display numbered questions with their **saved raw answer** and any different **derived value**. The coordinator selects a question number, sees its full wording, allowed numbered choices or editable text field, and the saved value as the Enter default. Enter leaves that answer unchanged; typing a choice number/text or new prose replaces only that answer. Text examples are labeled `Example (not saved)` and never become an answer by pressing Enter. `:back` returns to the question list, `:save` keeps edits without preparing, and `:cancel` discards only the current uncommitted field edit. Save a completed field edit atomically to the active private questionnaire, then recalculate affected derived fields, all 13 gates, and the GO/NO-GO preview. The gate list must show a visible `changed` marker and ask the coordinator to review the complete attributed answers before preparation. For participant assurance, selecting gate 5, 6, or 7 first shows shared-review questions and the six accepted contributions; selecting a contribution opens its exact host label, outcome, and any required separate-review details. Choosing a new gate or contribution never silently confirms the others.

The normal `D → 2` sign screen continues to show the exact canonical decision and evidence. It never silently replaces or signs the retired one. The signer receives only the new decision digest when the coordinator later chooses `D → 4`; the old decision and retirement record are not inserted into the signed evidence tree or archive.

## Eligibility and trust checks

Before showing the replacement confirmation, Relay must authenticate the signed V5 definition and exact current final-release checkpoint using the retained coordinator trust anchor and pinned verifier. It must identify the existing unsigned guided decision as local bytes bound to that exact ceremony/candidate/checkpoint, without rerunning pinned proof-tool verification of the decision being discarded. Require a valid saved **guided V2** questionnaire and preparation intent; deterministically expand the raw answers, regenerate the draft and reports, and compare their hashes with the intent, staged files, root draft, and canonical decision. These local comparisons establish which files are being preserved and detect off-guide changes; they do not approve the old decision. A manually prepared `D → 7` decision, missing or inconsistent intent, or unexpected public decision file is ineligible. If any byte is already damaged, stop for manual investigation instead of masking that damage with a new decision. The replacement decision must pass pinned proof-tool preparation and evidence checks against the signed final release before it can be promoted or signed.

Replacement is allowed only if the decision is a local, unsigned preparation and no dissemination may have begun. Require all of the following:

- No decision signature or signing-attempt record for any role, including an interrupted attempt with no output. Do not infer from a missing `.sig` that a key was never used.
- No decision AWS handoff manifest, transfer grant, upload/download receipt, or retained signer transport state for this decision; no prepared archive or publication record. Check `workflow-v4/decision-handoff/manifest.json`, transport receipts, `workflow-v4/publication/` and publication journals, and the private decision-grant directory at `../private-decision-grants/` outside the work folder. A failed or uncertain transfer blocks automatic replacement.
- No terminal decision checkpoint or other published decision reference, and no changed final-release checkpoint, policy, candidate, role assignment, or accepted-contribution list.
- The coordinator guide is the sole owner of the workspace lock; no concurrent Relay role command is running. The retirement location is absent, regular, inside the same work filesystem, and has enough space for bounded copies or an atomic directory move.

The checks cannot prove that a human did not manually copy the unsigned decision outside Relay. The UI states this limit and asks the coordinator to confirm it has not been circulated. If it was circulated, do not use this automatic path; a later formal supersession design must preserve and disclose the competing digest. No signature or remote AWS object is deleted to make eligibility pass.

## Durable state change

The existing public decision tree must never be overwritten. Relay creates a unique generation ID derived from the old decision digest and a fresh random attempt ID, and a private retirement directory under `workflow-v4/decision-replacements/<generation>/`, **outside** the active `workflow-v4/decision/` directory. Its bounded manifest records original paths, sizes, hashes, outcome, timestamp, authenticated final checkpoint, and the reason supplied by the operator. Inventory every fixed path produced by guided preparation: `ceremony/public/decision/`, `workflow-v4/decision-questionnaire.json`, `workflow-v4/decision/` (including `intent.json` and `staging/`), and root `decision-draft.json`. Require the root draft and staged draft to match the prepared decision's retained intent; a pre-existing unrelated manual draft blocks automatic retirement. The retirement manifest says `unsigned, locally superseded`; it does not claim that Relay can prove no off-machine copy exists. Keep the retirement directory outside `ceremony/public` and outside future archive inventories. Never place keys, AWS credentials, or private grants into the manifest.

Write and sync a durable replacement intent with the complete source/destination inventory *before* moving any file. After validating and hashing each source, rename it on the same filesystem to a fresh destination under that generation's retirement directory; never copy into a destination and then try to rename onto that populated directory. Move the canonical `ceremony/public/decision/` tree as one directory, then the private decision directory and the two fixed root sidecars. Sync both parent directories after each move. On restart, each listed item is either at its original path or at its retirement path with the recorded exact hash; a missing, duplicated, changed, or unexpected item stops recovery. Continue remaining moves only after this reconciliation. Mark retirement committed only when every original path is absent and every retired path matches the manifest. A central guard blocks **all** decision actions, including signing, handoff, packing, and `D → 7` manual recovery, while any replacement intent is uncommitted. Permit at most one uncommitted intent and resume it before accepting another `D → 8`. For subsequent replacements, scan the bounded committed manifest headers for unique generation IDs and old decision digests and the same ceremony/final checkpoint; fully rehash the current active generation and any in-progress retirement, not every unrelated earlier archive. Cap retained generations at 16 and aggregate retired bytes at 4 GiB; refuse further automatic replacement rather than silently pruning old files. These local manifests are integrity checks for recovery, not independently authenticated ceremony evidence. Create the next questionnaire only after the current retirement commits. A crash must never result in a new decision while an unclassified old public decision remains present. Use no recursive deletion as part of recovery.

The retired questionnaire, intent, stage, and draft retain their original bytes under the generation directory. Their fixed active paths are absent after commit, so the existing `D → 1` preparation may create a fresh active generation without changing all downstream paths. A committed retirement with no new active guided intent is an explicit `replacement awaiting questionnaire` state. In that state, show only `D → 1`/resume and save/exit; block `D → 7` and legacy publication paths even though `workflowV4GuidedDecisionActive()` alone would return false. Once the new active intent exists, `workflowV4GuidedDecisionActive()` refers to it. Existing `D → 2`, AWS handoff, and archive actions resolve only that active canonical decision and refuse an uncommitted retirement. Later preparation uses the existing pinned proof-tool `decision prepare` against the same signed final release, then promotes a fresh canonical `decision/` tree. Require at least one answer to change and require the new canonical decision digest to differ from every retired digest; a timestamp-only reissue is not a meaningful correction. It must not change any checkpoint or contribution file. Preserve the original decision digest only in the private retirement manifest; the new signed decision does not claim the old one was signed or published.

Do **not** add the local unsigned decision, its retirement, or replacement preparation to the ceremony activity journal or `relay audit export`. In particular, `D → 8` and the subsequent unsigned `D → 1` must not append a generic `workflow-v4/state.json` observation, call `beginAuditActivity`/`appendAuditActivity`, or add `recordDiagnostic` or legacy diagnostic events for these private actions. Their private recovery intent and retirement manifest supply only the state needed for crash-safe retry and inspection on that coordinator machine. Keep decision bytes, old/new draft digests, answers, and the reason for correction out of exported audit reports and diagnostic event payloads; the exporter reads both durable and legacy diagnostic sources, so test both. This does not erase audit records already produced by the existing software or conceal later signed or externally transferred decision activity. Keep the audit report's existing partial-coverage statement so its silence is not mistaken for a complete local history. Once a new decision is signed and packaged, the signed decision and its evidence remain verifiable in the public archive. The archive contains no retired unsigned decision or private recovery files.

## Implementation and compatibility

This is intended as a Relay-only coordinator guide and local-state change. Keep the original proof-tool image and signed definition pinned. Do not require participants or the release signer to upgrade merely to retire an unsigned coordinator draft; they must still receive and verify the **new** exact decision through the supported handoff. Retirement does not run proof-tool `decision prepare` or `decision verify` on the old unsigned decision. Confirm that pinned V5 `decision prepare`, `decision sign`, and `decision verify` accept the new canonical decision and that upgrade inventory treats the private retirement files as local observations, not public protocol evidence. If qualification finds a proof-tool or archive-format dependency, stop and revise the release plan rather than changing the frozen pin.

Also improve the normal questionnaire before another preparation: validate review-date syntax as it is entered; show which positive review answers will be downgraded because findings/checks/reviewer fields are missing; and show that `final.withhold = No` becomes `Unknown` when `final.findings` is `Not assessed`. These are usability diagnostics, not weaker GO gates. Preserve the honest `Unknown` path and every V5 gate.

The node-specific ceremony should not use this feature until it ships in an attested Relay release and the coordinator upgrade is qualified against a copy of its exact retained state. Qualification checks that the original release and signed update 26 remain identical, the first NO-GO decision is preserved, the new questionnaire can lead to either a truthful GO or NO-GO, and both signers see only the selected new decision digest. The already prepared decision must remain unsigned while waiting.

## Tests and qualification

1. Reproduce the current case with a complete signed V5 final release and an unsigned prepared NO-GO whose raw `final.withhold` is `No` but findings are `Not assessed`. Show the downgrade before preparation, retire it without a proof-tool rerun of the old decision, correct answers, prepare a different exact decision with the pinned proof-tool, and verify the old and new hashes independently. Assert no old-decision preparation or verification command is launched during retirement; assert the new decision is not promoted if its pinned preparation or evidence check fails.
2. Refuse replacement for coordinator/release-signer/auditor signatures, any signing-attempt record, AWS handoff/grant/uncertain upload (including a grant outside `work`), archive, terminal checkpoint, changed final-release checkpoint, altered old local bytes relative to their saved guided preparation, unexpected decision file, symlink, hardlink, path escape, duplicate generation, or another active guide.
3. Crash after intent creation and after each individual rename, including the canonical directory and fixed questionnaire, intent/stage, and draft paths. Exercise each absent/present combination and a second replacement following one committed retirement. Restart and prove byte-exact reconciliation or a clear blocked state, never two active canonical decisions or a regenerated decision using old fixed sidecars. Exit immediately after retirement commit and reopen before new `D → 1`; only guided questionnaire/resume may be offered, with `D → 7` and legacy publication blocked.
4. Recheck date parsing, missing findings, shared assurance coverage and per-contribution exceptions, incorrect circuit match, and every V5 gate. An entered `No` to the final withholding question must either remain `No` with established findings or be shown as downgraded before preparation. For each gate, test that `What remains for GO` identifies the actual blocking field, jumps to the correct question, and updates after an edit without treating a missing review as a formatting fix or claiming that a human review was mechanically verified. Test numeric question selection, Enter retaining a saved answer, editable examples remaining unsaved, `:back`, `:save`, `:cancel`, atomic field saves, resumption after interruption, and six contribution-specific paths.
5. Run the actual coordinator `start.sh` path against an isolated copy of the exact late-stage retained ceremony; verify that `D → 8` authenticates the signed release and retires the exact local old files without running proof-tool on the old decision, then that `D → 1` invokes pinned proof-tool preparation and evidence checks for the new decision. Continue through signing, signer handoff, required-signature verification, and archive packing for the replacement. Verify crash recovery from private state. Compare `relay audit export` before and after `D → 8`, interruption/restart, and unsigned replacement preparation using canary answers and digests; assert no new event, observation, path, or digest tied to either unsigned decision appears from the journal or either diagnostic source. Later signing and handoff may still appear through their existing audit paths. The public archive must contain the new signed decision only. Confirm the release/proof replay result and signed final checkpoint did not change. Test both a truthful GO and a still-PENDING NO-GO.

Open boundary: signed, circulated, or published decisions need a separate authenticated supersession protocol. This design intentionally does not claim that removing a local unsigned file can revoke a copy someone already received.

## Adversarial review disposition

The independent review found two initial blockers: fixed-path sidecars would have prevented a second preparation, and a copy-then-rename plan could not rename onto its populated destination. The design now inventories and retires every fixed preparation path with idempotent same-filesystem renames. It also requires proof that the first decision was guided V2 output, checks private grants outside the work folder, blocks every decision action during an interrupted retirement, and requires a changed answer and digest. The external-copy limit above remains unresolved by design; automatic replacement is unavailable once circulation is known or uncertain.
